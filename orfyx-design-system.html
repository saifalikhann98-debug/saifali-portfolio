01 — METHODOLOGY

Atomic structure

Every surface is composed from the same hierarchy: tokens feed atoms, atoms assemble into molecules, molecules into organisms — so a rebrand-level change is a variable edit, not a redraw.

·

Atoms

Buttons, inputs, toggles, checkboxes, pills, avatars — every interactive primitive with all of its states specified.

··

Molecules

Search bars, gym cards, stat tiles, list rows — small assemblies with one clear job, built only from atoms.

···

Organisms

Auth forms, checkout, the QR pass, dashboard modules — complete sections that drop into any screen.

06 — GOVERNANCE

Volt without shouting

The accent survives 80+ screens because usage is governed, not vibes-based.

Do

One volt CTA per screen — the action that moves money or opens a door.

Do

Volt for live signals — occupancy, QR countdown, active pass.

Don't

Volt backgrounds, volt text on cream, or two volt buttons in one view.

Don't

Volt for errors or warnings — failures get quiet reds and ambers.
