One design system

I built and own the design system every one of these products builds from — tokens, components and the developer handoff process. It's the single source of truth for 20+ engineers across the product suite.

The same system carried the white-label platforms built on BitDelta — DIFX, DoshFX, OFZA and Visiion — each launched in two weeks or less. Below, the three products live today: one balance-first home pattern, themed per brand.

Mobile

On mobile I designed the money-in flows end to end — buy crypto, fiat deposit, wallet onboarding and KYC: the screens every new user passes through before their first trade.
