The problem

Getting into a gym is heavier than it should be — tours, sales pitches, contracts, lock-ins. For anyone who moves between cities, or just wants to train without committing for a year, the membership model itself is the friction.

The approach

Orfyx turns it into a pass you request in seconds. Every gym is ID-verified before it goes live; you pick an activity and area, choose a pass — day, week or monthly — and walk in with a single QR scan. No paperwork, no lock-in, switch gyms mid-month.

On the other side, owners list a gym in about twenty minutes and verify entries with a one-handed scanner at reception — discovery and access without replacing their POS.

States & edge cases

The QR is the moment of truth — a doorway, often with weak signal, sometimes with a queue behind you. So every way it can fail was designed at full fidelity, on both sides of the door, each with one-tap recovery.

Design decisions

Verification before volume. Every gym is ID-verified before it goes live instead of open self-serve listings. Slower to grow — but one fake listing breaks the "walk straight in" promise, and the promise is the product.

The pass is the product. No accounts modelled on memberships — a pass you request in seconds, held as a QR that's offline-cached and wallet-ready, because the moment of truth is a doorway with weak signal.

Don't replace the POS. Owner tools stay deliberately small: list in about twenty minutes, validate entries with a one-handed scanner at reception. Asking gyms to swap their systems would have killed adoption before it started.

Information architecture

A two-sided product, so the IA splits by audience before it splits by feature — 80+ screens across two apps and the web. Members get browsing depth; owners get a shallow tree they can operate one-handed at a busy front desk.

User flow

Everything serves one loop — discover → buy → enter. The fork is payment: cash is a designed path, not a fallback, so a pass can sit pending until an owner validates it in person.

Outcome

Shipped. Designed and built end to end — the consumer flow, the owner tools and the digital pass — with native iOS and Android, offline-cached passes and wallet support. Built across Dubai and Bengaluru, live in the UAE and India.
